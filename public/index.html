Frequently Asked Questions

Why are 5 different ratio files needed?

Different frame standards have different aspect ratios (e.g. an 8x10 frame is 4:5, whereas a 24x36 poster is 2:3). Supplying all 5 ratio files ensures buyers can frame their prints in any store-bought frame without awkward cropping or distortion.

How does Digital Asset Forge handle Etsy's 20MB limit?

High-resolution 300 DPI master files often exceed 20MB in total. Digital Asset Forge automatically calculates file sizes and structures your bundle into compliant multi-part ZIP archives that fit Etsy's 5-file and 20MB per-file limits.

Can I execute this directly from Python or the terminal?

Yes. The repository includes an executable CLI (bin/digital-asset-forge), an Antigravity MCP stdio server, and a high-performance HTTP daemon on port 8118.
