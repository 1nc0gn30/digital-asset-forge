Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Pro features?

After completing checkout via Stripe or DePay Solana, your browser receives an activation parameter (?licensed=true) or you can paste your receipt order ID directly into the activation modal. All blurs and export limits dissolve instantly in your browser.

Why does Etsy have a 20MB limit?

Etsy restricts each digital attachment to a maximum of 20MB and a maximum of 5 files per listing. Digital Asset Forge automatically organizes your high-res 300 DPI master files into optimized multi-part ZIP bundles that stay safely under this threshold.

Can I use this for non-Etsy platforms?

Yes. The manifests, sizing taxonomies, and customer printing instructions are 100% platform-agnostic and work seamlessly with Shopify Digital Downloads, Gumroad, WooCommerce, and Patreon.
